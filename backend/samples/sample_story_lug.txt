Nze Nalongo Namubiru. Nazaalibwa mu Masaka mu mwaka gwa 1948. Taata wange yali ayitibwa Kizito, era yali mulimi wa mmwanyi. Maama wange Nakato yatusomesanga engero buli kiro ku kyoto. Bwe nnali muwala muto twagenda e Kampala okulaba Kabaka. Taata yatugamba nti, "Agali awamu ge galuma enyama." Mu biseera bya Amin twadduka ne tugenda ewa jjajja e Mpigi. Eyo gye nnasisinkana bba wange Ssemakula.
