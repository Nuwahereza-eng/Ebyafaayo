# Demo video plan (1 to 2 minutes)

Record the screen at 1280×800 with the browser zoomed to 110%. Prepare everything before recording, and cut the waiting time out in editing. Transcription and Gemma take minutes on a CPU; say so in a caption rather than hiding it.

**Before recording:** have one elder with two saved stories, so the remembrance page and timeline are full, and a fresh 30 to 45 second Luganda clip to record or upload live. Turn Wi-Fi off and show the network icon: this is the point of the project.

| Time | Screen | Voice-over / caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Home page, the elders' cards | "Our grandparents' stories are disappearing. Ebyafaayo keeps them, in their own words, in Luganda, on our own laptop." |
| 0:10–0:15 | Wi-Fi turned off | "No internet. Nothing leaves this computer." |
| 0:15–0:30 | Record screen: choose the elder, read the consent, tick it, press Record | "First we ask for consent, and the app stores it with the recording." |
| 0:30–0:45 | Review screen: progress bar, then the transcript next to the audio player; fix one word | "Sunbird AI's open Luganda speech model writes it down. We listen and correct it, because the elder's words matter." Caption: "about N minutes on a 2017 laptop CPU (sped up)". |
| 0:45–1:10 | "Turn into a family memory": Gemma's output. Hover over a person to show the source sentence. Show an amber "not found" item or the "never guess a clan" note | "Gemma 4 runs locally through Ollama. It translates, finds the people, places and times, and quotes the sentence each came from. If it cannot point to the sentence, we flag it. It never guesses a clan or totem." |
| 1:10–1:20 | Edit a relationship, then Save | "The family has the final word on everything." |
| 1:20–1:35 | Remembrance page: timeline, people, questions to ask next time | "Every story builds a remembrance page, with gentle questions to ask Jjajja next time." |
| 1:35–1:50 | Memory book, then "Save as PDF" | "And a memory book we can print and share at the next family gathering." |
| 1:50–2:00 | About page: the open models and credits | "Open models: Sunbird ASR and Gemma 4. Free, private, and in our language." |
